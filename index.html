About Us

We Build Anything in 3D

DotProject was born from a passion for turning digital ideas into physical reality. Based in Dhaka, we operate modern FDM and resin 3D printers to manufacture unique, functional, and beautiful products.

Whether it's a personalized keychain, a decorative shelf piece, or a full smart device enclosure — we design, print, and ship it straight to you.

  • Premium PLA+, PETG & resin materials
  • Free design consultation for custom orders
  • CAD modelling service available
  • Bulk & B2B orders welcome
  • Based in Dhaka, ship nationwide
